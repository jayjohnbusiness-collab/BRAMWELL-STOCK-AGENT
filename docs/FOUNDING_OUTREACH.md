# Founding-Member Outreach (Phase 0)

How to turn early-access signups into the 5–10 paying, engaged founding users
who clear the gate into Phase 1. This is the **concierge MVP (Wizard-of-Oz)**:
you deliver the Concierge experience *by hand* to a few people, at a founding
rate, before building any of it. It validates two things at once — will they
pay $100-ish, and will they stay engaged.

Send the email below within a day of someone joining the list, while intent is
warm. Personalize the bracketed bits. Keep it short — it's a founder writing to
an early believer, not a marketing blast.

## Running the concierge MVP

For each founding member:
- **Onboard (10 min):** their tickers, why they hold them, what "worth it" means
  to them, and when/how they want to hear from you (morning? on-event? call, text,
  or voice note?).
- **Deliver daily:** a short spoken briefing (record a voice note) covering their
  movers, anything that crossed a level, and earnings/news on their names. Add an
  ad-hoc heads-up when something material breaks.
- **Charge the founding rate** from week one (or after a single free briefing).
  Willingness to actually pay is the signal — a free "yes" is worth little.
- **Log what they ask for.** Every "can you also…" is a prioritized feature for
  the real build. This is the most valuable output of Phase 0.

Keep it to ~5–10 people. The point isn't scale — it's proof.

---

## Reading the signal

Your Google Form responses (Responses tab, or the linked Sheet) are the top of
the funnel. Two things to watch:

- **Volume + rate:** how many sign up relative to visits. A trickle of the right
  people beats a flood of the wrong ones, but you need enough to fill 5–10 seats.
- **"What would make it worth $100/mo?"** — this is the hero-feature vote. If it's
  overwhelmingly *"the voice squawk that calls me,"* that's your build order
  confirmed. If it skews to *"AI analyst"* or *"portfolio risk,"* let that reorder
  the roadmap. Don't assume — let the answers tell you which of the must-have four
  leads.

### The gate into Phase 1
Do **not** start the backend build until:
- a credible, repeatable stream of signups (not a one-off spike), **and**
- **≥5 founding members** who pay the founding rate and stay engaged for **4+
  weeks** of hand-delivered briefings.

If you can't fill 5 hand-delivered seats, no amount of engineering fixes that —
revisit the price, the promise, or the audience first. If you *can*, you've
proven demand with almost no code, and Phase 1 is worth funding.
